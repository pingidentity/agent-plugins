# PingOne for Gift Card Redemption

## Overview
PingOne for Gift Card Redemption is a PingOne and DaVinci solution for safer gift-card redemption journeys. It combines stronger verification with customer self-service updates to help limit unauthorized access and related fraud.

## Core Concepts
- **Prebuilt DaVinci flows:** Packaged onboarding-oriented flows provide a starting point for common redemption journeys.
- **Risk-based protection:** Threat-level assessment helps identify suspicious activity and support proportionate verification.
- **Threat signals:** Detection for automated bots, temporary email services, and adversary-in-the-middle threats informs fraud defenses.
- **Step-up authentication:** MFA, notifications, and additional verification strengthen account access during redemption.
- **Flexible authentication:** Magic links, email or SMS codes, FIDO2, voice, mobile apps, and TOTP support different customer needs.
- **Profile self-service:** End users can change their email address and other profile details as part of the journey.

## When to use
- Gift-card redemption needs risk assessment and stronger authentication.
- Customers need to update profile details during redemption.

## When not to use
- Do not treat the packaged flows as a complete fit for every scenario; review limitations and customization needs with Ping Identity.
- Redirect broader identity journeys to the relevant PingOne or DaVinci capabilities.
