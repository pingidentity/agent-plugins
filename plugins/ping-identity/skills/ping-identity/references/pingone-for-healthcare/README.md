# PingOne for Healthcare

## Overview
PingOne for Healthcare is a PingOne and DaVinci flow pack for patient registration, sign-on, and support. It combines identity verification, authentication choices, and threat detection to protect access.

## Core Concepts
- **Patient registration and sign-on:** Supports patient account creation and authentication journeys for healthcare users.
- **Identity verification:** Verifies identity during registration and progressively during authentication as risk changes.
- **Threat detection:** Identifies bots, disposable email addresses, and adversary-in-the-middle attacks to help reduce account-takeover risk.
- **Risk-based decisions:** Classifies activity as high, medium, or low threat for risk-appropriate responses.
- **Authentication and support:** Offers email magic links, email or SMS one-time passwords, FIDO2, voice, mobile applications, and TOTP; a CSR flow supports account enablement and password resets.

## When to use
- Use for patient-facing registration, sign-on, and account-recovery scenarios that need healthcare-oriented flows.
- Use when customer service representatives need a flow for account management assistance.

## When not to use
- Do not treat the templates as a complete implementation for every business, compliance, or regulatory requirement.
- For specialized requirements, adapt the flows and review them with a Ping Identity representative.
